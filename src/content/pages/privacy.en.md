## Privacy policy

<p class="dim">Last updated: [DATE]</p>

### The short version

The Pockio website does not use cookies, trackers or analytics. It does not ask you for any personal data.

### What the server sees

The site is hosted as static files. Like any web server, the hosting provider may keep technical logs (for example your IP address and the time of the request) for security and operation. I do not have access to them in any way that identifies you, and I do not use them.

### Email

If you write to me, I get your email address and your message. I use them only to reply, and I do not share them with anyone.

### Apps

Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from its page. This page covers only the website.

### Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new date.
