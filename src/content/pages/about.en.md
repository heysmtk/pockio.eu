## Pockio is made by one person, in the evenings

I’m Tomáš. Pockio is my small studio for pocket-sized mobile utilities. I run it in the evenings, next to work and family, which is why the apps are small, simple and well thought out.

### Why my own studio

I’ve always wanted to make my own apps. Pockio is how I turn ideas for things that would be handy in your pocket into real apps.

### Where the name comes from

**pocket + studio = Pockio**. A studio of small things you always have at hand.

### How the apps are made

- An idea from everyday life.
- One feature. An app does one thing.
- A test on my own phone first.
- Google Play. I release it and improve it based on feedback.
